SSH certificates for Linux sysadmins who know SSH keys and are new to certificate authorities. Learn how servers verify users and laptops verify hosts.

Follow the setup in PKI Manager, Oriol Rius's open-source tool for X.509 and SSH certificate authorities.

19 episodes, 1-2 minutes each, about 26 minutes total. A synthetic voice narrates the series. Every episode has English subtitles.
